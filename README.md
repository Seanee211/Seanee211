# Soojin Lee

**Human-centered AI for evidence, context, and judgment**

I study how interactive AI can support reasoning, inquiry, and decision making while preserving human judgment. I am a Visiting Researcher at iLab, Sungkyunkwan University, and hold an M.S. in Data Science from Kyungpook National University.

My research connects organizational experience reuse, applied machine learning, and interfaces that help people examine the information behind a recommendation.

## Research

- **AI-mediated mentoring:** Investigating how prior organizational experience can inform a new situation while preserving the context needed to judge its relevance. A paper is accepted to HICSS-60; a separate manuscript is in preparation.
- **Clinical machine learning:** Co-first author of a [published study of recurrent lumbar disc herniation](https://doi.org/10.3340/jkns.2025.0120), contributing to the modeling pipeline, analysis, and writing.
- **Data utilization opportunities:** Exploring connections between existing data and potential applications in emerging technologies. Manuscript submitted.

## Selected public work

- [STEEL CUT / ScrapPrice_Prediction](https://github.com/Seanee211/ScrapPrice_Prediction): A historical team project on scrap-price forecasting and an interface for inspecting predictions and supporting data. I served as project manager on a five-person team.
- [Data science learning projects](https://github.com/Seanee211/DataScience-AI_MiniProjects): Earlier coursework and exploratory projects from my transition into data science.
